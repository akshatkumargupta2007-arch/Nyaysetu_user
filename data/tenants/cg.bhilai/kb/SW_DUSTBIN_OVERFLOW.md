# Overflowing public dustbin (डस्टबिन भरा हुआ)

**Category code:** `SW_DUSTBIN_OVERFLOW` (group: SOLID_WASTE)

**Definition:** Overflowing public dustbin — reported by citizens as डस्टबिन भरा हुआ.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** overflowing, public, dustbin

**General words for the SOLID_WASTE group:** kachra, कचरा, कूड़ा, safai, सफाई, dustbin, garbage

**Disambiguation:** Household/street garbage collection — not medical waste (see PUBLIC_HEALTH) and not a blocked drain (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "डस्टबिन भरा हुआ है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (overflowing public dustbin, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan overflowing public dustbin ki problem hai, kai din se."
