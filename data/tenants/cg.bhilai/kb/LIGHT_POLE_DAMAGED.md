**Category code:** `LIGHT_POLE_DAMAGED` (group: STREETLIGHTS)

**Definition:** Light pole damaged / leaning — reported by citizens as खम्बा टेढ़ा/क्षतिग्रस्त.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** light, pole, damaged, leaning

**General words for the STREETLIGHTS group:** streetlight, street light, batti, बत्ती, khamba, खम्बा, light
