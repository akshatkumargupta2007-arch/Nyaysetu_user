# Construction debris on road (मलबा सड़क पर)

**Category code:** `ROAD_DEBRIS` (group: ROADS_FOOTPATHS)

**Definition:** Construction debris on road — reported by citizens as मलबा सड़क पर.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** construction, debris, road

**General words for the ROADS_FOOTPATHS group:** sadak, सड़क, road, footpath, फुटपाथ

**Disambiguation:** The road or footpath surface itself — a streetlight on the same road is STREETLIGHTS, not this.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "मलबा सड़क पर है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (construction debris on road, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan construction debris on road ki problem hai, kai din se."
