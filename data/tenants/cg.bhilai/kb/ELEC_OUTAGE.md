# Power outage / blackout (बिजली गुल)

**Category code:** `ELEC_OUTAGE` (group: ELECTRICITY)

**Definition:** Power outage / blackout — reported by citizens as बिजली गुल.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** batti gul, बत्ती गुल, power cut, blackout, gul

**General words for the ELECTRICITY group:** bijli, बिजली, current, transformer, ट्रांसफार्मर

**Disambiguation:** The electricity supply/grid (CSPDCL) — a streetlight pole specifically is STREETLIGHTS even though both involve wires.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "बिजली गुल है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (power outage / blackout, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan power outage / blackout ki problem hai, kai din se."
