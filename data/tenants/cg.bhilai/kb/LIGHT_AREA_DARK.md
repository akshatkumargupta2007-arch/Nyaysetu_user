# Entire street / area dark (पूरी गली अंधेरे में)

**Category code:** `LIGHT_AREA_DARK` (group: STREETLIGHTS)

**Definition:** Entire street / area dark — reported by citizens as पूरी गली अंधेरे में.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** andhera, अंधेरा, andhere mein, poora mohalla

**General words for the STREETLIGHTS group:** streetlight, street light, batti, बत्ती, khamba, खम्बा, light

**Disambiguation:** Municipal street lighting poles/fixtures — a general area power cut is ELECTRICITY instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** crime_risk

**Example complaints:**
- "पूरी गली अंधेरे में है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (entire street / area dark, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan entire street / area dark ki problem hai, kai din se."
