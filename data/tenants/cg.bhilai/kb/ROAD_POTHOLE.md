# Pothole on road (सड़क पर गड्ढा)

**Category code:** `ROAD_POTHOLE` (group: ROADS_FOOTPATHS)

**Definition:** Pothole on road — reported by citizens as सड़क पर गड्ढा.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** gaddha, gadda, गड्ढा, pothole, pot hole

**General words for the ROADS_FOOTPATHS group:** sadak, सड़क, road, footpath, फुटपाथ

**Disambiguation:** The road or footpath surface itself — a streetlight on the same road is STREETLIGHTS, not this.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "सड़क पर गड्ढा है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (pothole on road, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan pothole on road ki problem hai, kai din se."
