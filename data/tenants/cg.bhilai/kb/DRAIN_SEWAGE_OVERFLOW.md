# Sewage overflowing on road (सीवर ओवरफ्लो)

**Category code:** `DRAIN_SEWAGE_OVERFLOW` (group: SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE)

**Definition:** Sewage overflowing on road — reported by citizens as सीवर ओवरफ्लो.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** overflow, ओवरफ्लो, behna, बहना

**General words for the SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE group:** nali, naali, नाली, gutter, गटर, sewer, सीवर

**Disambiguation:** Underground sewer lines and surface storm-water drains — if the complaint is about no water coming out of a tap, that is WATER_SUPPLY instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** hygiene_hazard

**Example complaints:**
- "सीवर ओवरफ्लो है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (sewage overflowing on road, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan sewage overflowing on road ki problem hai, kai din se."
