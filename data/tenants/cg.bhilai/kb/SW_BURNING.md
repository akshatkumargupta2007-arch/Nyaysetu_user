# Burning of garbage/leaves (कचरा जलाना)

**Category code:** `SW_BURNING` (group: SOLID_WASTE)

**Definition:** Burning of garbage/leaves — reported by citizens as कचरा जलाना.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** burning, garbage, leaves

**General words for the SOLID_WASTE group:** kachra, कचरा, कूड़ा, safai, सफाई, dustbin, garbage

**Disambiguation:** Household/street garbage collection — not medical waste (see PUBLIC_HEALTH) and not a blocked drain (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).

**Safety hazards to watch for:** air_quality

**Example complaints:**
- "कचरा जलाना है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (burning of garbage/leaves, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan burning of garbage/leaves ki problem hai, kai din se."
