# Blind spot / missing signage (स्पीड ब्रेकर का बोर्ड नहीं)

**Category code:** `TRAFFIC_MISSING_SIGNAGE` (group: TRAFFIC_TRANSPORT)

**Definition:** Blind spot / missing signage — reported by citizens as स्पीड ब्रेकर का बोर्ड नहीं.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** blind, spot, missing, signage

**General words for the TRAFFIC_TRANSPORT group:** traffic, ट्रैफिक, sadak, सड़क

**Disambiguation:** Traffic signals, parking, abandoned vehicles — a damaged road surface is ROADS_FOOTPATHS instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** accident_risk

**Example complaints:**
- "स्पीड ब्रेकर का बोर्ड नहीं है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (blind spot / missing signage, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan blind spot / missing signage ki problem hai, kai din se."
