# Tree fallen on road/property (पेड़ गिर गया)

**Category code:** `PARK_TREE_FALLEN` (group: PARKS_RECREATION)

**Definition:** Tree fallen on road/property — reported by citizens as पेड़ गिर गया.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** tree, fallen, road, property

**General words for the PARKS_RECREATION group:** park, पार्क, ped, पेड़, tree
