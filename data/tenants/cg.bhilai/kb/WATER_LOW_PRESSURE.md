# Low water pressure (पानी का प्रेशर कम)

**Category code:** `WATER_LOW_PRESSURE` (group: WATER_SUPPLY)

**Definition:** Low water pressure — reported by citizens as पानी का प्रेशर कम.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** water, pressure

**General words for the WATER_SUPPLY group:** pani, पानी, nal, नल, pipeline, पाइपलाइन, supply

**Disambiguation:** The piped water supply to homes — not a blocked drain or sewage overflow (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "पानी का प्रेशर कम है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (low water pressure, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan low water pressure ki problem hai, kai din se."
