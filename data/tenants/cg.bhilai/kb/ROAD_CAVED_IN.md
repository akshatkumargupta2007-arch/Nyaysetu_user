# Road caved in / sinkhole (सड़क धंस गई)

**Category code:** `ROAD_CAVED_IN` (group: ROADS_FOOTPATHS)

**Definition:** Road caved in / sinkhole — reported by citizens as सड़क धंस गई.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** dhans gaya, धंस गया, sinkhole, caved in

**General words for the ROADS_FOOTPATHS group:** sadak, सड़क, road, footpath, फुटपाथ

**Disambiguation:** The road or footpath surface itself — a streetlight on the same road is STREETLIGHTS, not this.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** collapse_risk

**Example complaints:**
- "सड़क धंस गई है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (road caved in / sinkhole, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan road caved in / sinkhole ki problem hai, kai din se."
