**Category code:** `SW_DEAD_ANIMAL` (group: SOLID_WASTE)

**Definition:** Dead animal on street — reported by citizens as सड़क पर मरा जानवर.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** dead, animal, street

**General words for the SOLID_WASTE group:** kachra, कचरा, कूड़ा, safai, सफाई, dustbin, garbage

**Disambiguation:** Household/street garbage collection — not medical waste (see PUBLIC_HEALTH) and not a blocked drain (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).
