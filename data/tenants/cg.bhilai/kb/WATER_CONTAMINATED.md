# Contaminated / muddy water (गंदा/दूषित पानी)

**Category code:** `WATER_CONTAMINATED` (group: WATER_SUPPLY)

**Definition:** Contaminated / muddy water — reported by citizens as गंदा/दूषित पानी.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** contaminated, muddy, water

**General words for the WATER_SUPPLY group:** pani, पानी, nal, नल, pipeline, पाइपलाइन, supply

**Disambiguation:** The piped water supply to homes — not a blocked drain or sewage overflow (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).

**Safety hazards to watch for:** disease_risk

**Example complaints:**
- "गंदा/दूषित पानी है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (contaminated / muddy water, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan contaminated / muddy water ki problem hai, kai din se."
