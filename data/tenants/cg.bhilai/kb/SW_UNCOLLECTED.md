# Garbage uncollected (कचरा नहीं उठा)

**Category code:** `SW_UNCOLLECTED` (group: SOLID_WASTE)

**Definition:** Garbage uncollected — reported by citizens as कचरा नहीं उठा.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** nahi utha, नहीं उठा, uncollected, din se padi

**General words for the SOLID_WASTE group:** kachra, कचरा, कूड़ा, safai, सफाई, dustbin, garbage

**Disambiguation:** Household/street garbage collection — not medical waste (see PUBLIC_HEALTH) and not a blocked drain (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "कचरा नहीं उठा है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (garbage uncollected, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan garbage uncollected ki problem hai, kai din se."
