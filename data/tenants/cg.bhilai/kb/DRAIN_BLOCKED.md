# Blocked storm-water drain (नाली जाम)

**Category code:** `DRAIN_BLOCKED` (group: SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE)

**Definition:** Blocked storm-water drain — reported by citizens as नाली जाम.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** jam, जाम, blocked, block, choked, nali jam, नाली जाम

**General words for the SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE group:** nali, naali, नाली, gutter, गटर, sewer, सीवर

**Disambiguation:** Underground sewer lines and surface storm-water drains — if the complaint is about no water coming out of a tap, that is WATER_SUPPLY instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** flood_risk

**Example complaints:**
- "नाली जाम है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (blocked storm-water drain, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan blocked storm-water drain ki problem hai, kai din se."
