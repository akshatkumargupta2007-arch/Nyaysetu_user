# No water supply (पानी नहीं आ रहा)

**Category code:** `WATER_NO_SUPPLY` (group: WATER_SUPPLY)

**Definition:** No water supply — reported by citizens as पानी नहीं आ रहा.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** pani nahi, पानी नहीं, no water, sookha

**General words for the WATER_SUPPLY group:** pani, पानी, nal, नल, pipeline, पाइपलाइन, supply

**Disambiguation:** The piped water supply to homes — not a blocked drain or sewage overflow (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "पानी नहीं आ रहा है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (no water supply, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan no water supply ki problem hai, kai din se."
