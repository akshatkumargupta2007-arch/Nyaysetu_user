# Illegal discharge into lake/river (नाले में अवैध रसायन)

**Category code:** `DRAIN_ILLEGAL_DISCHARGE` (group: SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE)

**Definition:** Illegal discharge into lake/river — reported by citizens as नाले में अवैध रसायन.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** illegal, discharge, into, lake, river

**General words for the SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE group:** nali, naali, नाली, gutter, गटर, sewer, सीवर

**Disambiguation:** Underground sewer lines and surface storm-water drains — if the complaint is about no water coming out of a tap, that is WATER_SUPPLY instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** pollution

**Example complaints:**
- "नाले में अवैध रसायन है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (illegal discharge into lake/river, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan illegal discharge into lake/river ki problem hai, kai din se."
