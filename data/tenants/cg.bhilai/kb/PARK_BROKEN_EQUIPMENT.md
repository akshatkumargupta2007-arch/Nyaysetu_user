# Broken playground equipment (टूटा झूला/उपकरण)

**Category code:** `PARK_BROKEN_EQUIPMENT` (group: PARKS_RECREATION)

**Definition:** Broken playground equipment — reported by citizens as टूटा झूला/उपकरण.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** broken, playground, equipment

**General words for the PARKS_RECREATION group:** park, पार्क, ped, पेड़, tree

**Disambiguation:** Public parks, playgrounds and trees — a tree touching a power line is still filed here but flagged as an electrical hazard too.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** child_safety
