# Open / missing manhole cover (खुला मैनहोल)

**Category code:** `DRAIN_OPEN_MANHOLE` (group: SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE)

**Definition:** Open / missing manhole cover — reported by citizens as खुला मैनहोल.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** manhole, मैनहोल, dhakkan, ढक्कन, gutter cover

**General words for the SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE group:** nali, naali, नाली, gutter, गटर, sewer, सीवर

**Disambiguation:** Underground sewer lines and surface storm-water drains — if the complaint is about no water coming out of a tap, that is WATER_SUPPLY instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** fall_hazard, child_safety

**Example complaints:**
- "खुला मैनहोल है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (open / missing manhole cover, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan open / missing manhole cover ki problem hai, kai din se."
