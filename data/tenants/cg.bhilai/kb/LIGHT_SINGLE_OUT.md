# Single streetlight not working (एक स्ट्रीटलाइट बंद)

**Category code:** `LIGHT_SINGLE_OUT` (group: STREETLIGHTS)

**Definition:** Single streetlight not working — reported by citizens as एक स्ट्रीटलाइट बंद.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** band, बंद, kharab, खराब

**General words for the STREETLIGHTS group:** streetlight, street light, batti, बत्ती, khamba, खम्बा, light

**Disambiguation:** Municipal street lighting poles/fixtures — a general area power cut is ELECTRICITY instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "एक स्ट्रीटलाइट बंद है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (single streetlight not working, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan single streetlight not working ki problem hai, kai din se."
