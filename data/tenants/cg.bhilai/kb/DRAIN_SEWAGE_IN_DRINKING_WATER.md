# Sewage mixing in drinking water (पीने के पानी में सीवेज मिक्स)

**Category code:** `DRAIN_SEWAGE_IN_DRINKING_WATER` (group: SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE)

**Definition:** Sewage mixing in drinking water — reported by citizens as पीने के पानी में सीवेज मिक्स.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** sewage, mixing, drinking, water

**General words for the SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE group:** nali, naali, नाली, gutter, गटर, sewer, सीवर

**Disambiguation:** Underground sewer lines and surface storm-water drains — if the complaint is about no water coming out of a tap, that is WATER_SUPPLY instead.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** biohazard, disease_risk
