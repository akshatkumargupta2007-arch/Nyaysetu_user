# Encroachment in park (पार्क में अतिक्रमण)

**Category code:** `PARK_ENCROACHMENT` (group: PARKS_RECREATION)

**Definition:** Encroachment in park — reported by citizens as पार्क में अतिक्रमण.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** encroachment, park

**General words for the PARKS_RECREATION group:** park, पार्क, ped, पेड़, tree

**Disambiguation:** Public parks, playgrounds and trees — a tree touching a power line is still filed here but flagged as an electrical hazard too.

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "पार्क में अतिक्रमण है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (encroachment in park, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan encroachment in park ki problem hai, kai din se."
