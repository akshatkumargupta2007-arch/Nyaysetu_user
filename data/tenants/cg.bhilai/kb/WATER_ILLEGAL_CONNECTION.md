# Illegal water connection (अवैध पानी कनेक्शन)

**Category code:** `WATER_ILLEGAL_CONNECTION` (group: WATER_SUPPLY)

**Definition:** Illegal water connection — reported by citizens as अवैध पानी कनेक्शन.

**Specific local words for THIS subcategory:** illegal, water, connection

**General words for the WATER_SUPPLY group:** pani, पानी, nal, नल, pipeline, पाइपलाइन, supply

**Disambiguation:** The piped water supply to homes — not a blocked drain or sewage overflow (see SEWERAGE_DRAINAGE).

**Safety hazards to watch for:** none

**Example complaints:**
- "अवैध पानी कनेक्शन है, जल्दी ठीक करवाइए।" (illegal water connection, please fix it soon)
- "Yahan illegal water connection ki problem hai, kai din se."
